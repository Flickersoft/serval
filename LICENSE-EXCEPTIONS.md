## 1. Distribution through application stores

As an additional permission under section 7 of the AGPLv3, the Licensor grants permission to
convey the Program, or a work based on it, through an application distribution platform —
including but not limited to the Apple App Store and Google Play — notwithstanding the terms

section 6 or section 10 of the AGPLv3.

This covers, in particular:

* **Section 6 (conveying non-source forms), and its Installation Information requirement for
  User Products.** A platform may sign, encrypt or otherwise restrict the installed application
  so that a recipient cannot install a modified version on their own device.
* **Section 10 (no further restrictions).** A platform may impose terms of service on the
  recipient — device or account limits, digital rights management, or a prohibition on

### Condition

This permission applies only while the complete corresponding source of the conveyed version
remains publicly available under the AGPLv3, at no charge, and the application identifies from
within its own interface where that source can be obtained.

A version conveyed through an application store that does not meet this condition is conveyed
without this permission, and section 6 and section 10 apply to it unmodified.

### Why this exists

The AGPLv3 forbids conveying the Program under terms that restrict what a recipient may do with
it. Application stores require exactly such terms. Without this permission the two cannot both
be satisfied, and Serval could not ship a mobile application at all.
